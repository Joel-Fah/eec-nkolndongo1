# Add a repeating event

## What it is

A **recurring event** is a meeting that comes back (a rehearsal, a monthly gathering). You create it **once** with its repeat rule; the site works out all the coming dates.

## Step by step

1. Open **Agenda**, then **Créer un événement** (create an event).
2. Enter the title, and the date and time of the first occurrence.
3. Under **Répétition** (repeat), choose: every week, a given weekday of the month (e.g. the 1st Sunday), or a date of the month (e.g. the 15th).
4. Choose when the repetition ends, then **Publier**.

## Cancel or move a single date

1. Open the event and find the date in the list.
2. Click **Annuler cette date** (it shows as "Annulé" on the site) or **Déplacer** to give a new date and time.

The other dates do not change.
