# Fill in "This Sunday"

The **This Sunday** card on the home page shows the theme, the readings and, if the people agree, who preaches.

1. Open **Liturgie**, tab **Dimanches**. Sundays **to prepare** are at the top.
2. Pick the Sunday, then fill in the **theme** and the **readings** (**Ajouter une lecture**).
3. For names (preacher, liturgist): tick the consent box **only if these people agreed** to have their name shown.
4. Click **Publier**.

## Good to know

- **Reprendre la semaine précédente** (copy last week) copies the readings but never the names: the agreement was for one Sunday.
- Bible texts shown come from the Louis Segond 1910 (French) and the World English Bible (English).
