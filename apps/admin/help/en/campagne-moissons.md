# Follow the Harvest festival

## What it is

A Harvest festival **campaign** sets a **goal** (in FCFA) and **dates**. The site shows a **basket that fills up** as the amounts received are **validated**. Two people are involved: the **treasurer** enters the amounts, **someone else** (the secretariat) validates them. Only the **validated total** is public: never a single amount, never a name.

## Step by step

1. **Secretariat**: open **Harvest festival**, then **New campaign**. The dates come from Settings; enter the **goal**, a short introduction, a photo if you like, then **Publish**.
2. **Treasurer**: **Amounts received** tab. One line per amount (for example a Sunday's total) with its date. The group is optional. **Never write a donor's name**, not even in the internal note.
3. **Secretariat**: check each line against reality, then click **Validate**. You cannot validate an amount you entered yourself.
4. Optional: **Groups** tab, to give a group a goal. It is encouragement: the site lists groups alphabetically, with no ranking.

## Good to know

- If you change the amount of an already validated entry, its validation is removed: validate it again.
- The site receives **no payment**: the ways to give shown are the ones validated in Settings.
- The treasurer and the secretariat need these rights: give them in **Roles** (treasurer: read and enter; secretariat: read, edit and validate).
