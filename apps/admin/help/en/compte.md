# My account

## What it is

**My account** gathers everything about you: your identity, your profile as a member of the parish, your password, your preferences and the devices you are signed in on. Team members belong to the community too, so the profile is completed here, as for any member.

## Complete my profile

1. In **My full profile**, read what is asked and why, tick the box (it is never ticked for you), then **Turn on my full profile**.
2. Fill in what you wish: birth date, gender, neighbourhood, city, marital status, profession. Everything is optional and can be changed.
3. Add a photo if you like. The team who manage members see it; the public never does. If you sign in with Google, your Google photo is already there: keep it or choose another.
4. With **Use my photo as my avatar**, your photo becomes your avatar in the menu. Without it, a generated avatar represents you.

> Withdrawing your agreement erases your details and your photo.

## Password and sign-in

- If you sign in with **email and password**, change the password here; your other devices are signed out.
- If you sign in with **Google**, there is no password to change.
- Under **Devices**, you can close a session you do not recognise.


## Sign in with Google too

You can link your Google account to sign in with it, **as well as** your password (which keeps working).

1. Under **Sign in with Google**, type your password to confirm, then **Link my Google account**.
2. Choose your Google account. It can have a different address from this account’s.
3. When you come back, Google is linked: on the sign-in page, **Continue with Google** lets you in.

- Only the signed-in person can link Google. The secretariat cannot do it for you, and Google is never linked by the e-mail address alone.
- **Remove Google** detaches it at any time. If Google is your only way in, ask the secretariat for a reset link to choose a password before removing it.
- If you signed up with Google before you had a team role, Google keeps working: sign in with Google again after the role change. The admin then asks you to **choose a password** (a back-up way in, and a protected account even if your role changes later). Your rights come back as soon as it is saved.

## Good to know

- A small **To complete** mark next to your avatar reminds you that the profile is not finished. It blocks nothing.
- Your language and theme (light, dark) are set under **Preferences**.
