# Import the meditation programme

The Bible service hands out a calendar every year: for each day, readings and a psalm. Putting it on the site takes three steps.

## Step by step

1. Open **Méditations**, then **Nouveau programme** (new programme): give it a title (for example “Meditations 2026”) and the first and last day.
2. In the programme, tab **Importer un calendrier** (import a calendar): pick the **CSV** file (or paste its content) and click **Vérifier** (check).
3. Read the report. **Errors** (a wrong date, a duplicated day) block the import: the **line** is shown. **Warnings** block nothing but deserve a look.
4. Click **Importer**, then **Publier** the programme.

## The file

Columns read: **Date** (2026-07-01), **Headline** (“Christmas”, “1st Sunday of Advent”…), **Reading 1/2/3 Label** and **Ref**, **Psalm**. An ordinary day has one reading and a psalm; a Sunday or feast up to three readings. The “Day”, “Reflection Theme” and “Status” columns are ignored.

## Fix a day

In the **Jours** tab, open the day, change the passage or the psalm, then **Enregistrer**. You can also pick a **verse of the day** among the readings and type its text (Louis Segond 1910 in French, World English Bible in English): it then replaces the home page verse for that day.

> The site only shows the **references** of the readings, never the text, except for the verse of the day you typed.
