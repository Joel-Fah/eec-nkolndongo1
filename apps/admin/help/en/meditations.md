# Import the meditation programme

## What it is

A **meditation programme** is the reading calendar of a period: for each day, one to three readings and a psalm, as the Bible service prints them. The site draws “Today’s meditation”, the week and the month from it. You create it once, then import the year’s calendar (CSV file) or add days by hand.

## Step by step

1. Open **Meditations**, then **New programme**: give it a title (for example “Meditations 2026”) and the first and last day.
2. In the programme, tab **Import a calendar**: click **Download the template** to see the format, then pick your **CSV** file (or paste its content) and click **Check**.
3. Read the **preview**: one row per day, with its status. An **error** (wrong date, a chapter or verse that does not exist) is flagged on its row and that row is not imported; the others are. **Warnings** block nothing but deserve a look.
4. Click **Import**, then **Publish** the programme.

## The file

Columns read: **Date** (2026-07-01), **Headline** (“Christmas”, “1st Sunday of Advent”…), **Reading 1/2/3 Label** and **Ref**, **Psalm**. An ordinary day has one reading and a psalm; a Sunday or feast up to three readings. The “Day”, “Reflection Theme” and “Status” columns are ignored.

## Fix a day

In the **Jours** tab, open the day, change the passage or the psalm, then **Enregistrer**. You can also pick a **verse of the day** among the readings and type its text (Louis Segond 1910 in French, World English Bible in English): it then replaces the home page verse for that day.

> The site only shows the **references** of the readings, never the text, except for the verse of the day you typed.

## Edit a day

- Click a day to open it. Each **reading** is entered by **book**, **chapter**, **From** and **To** (verses): a chapter or verse that does not exist cannot be typed. The **kind** (Old Testament, Epistle, Gospel…) follows the book; you can change it.
- The **psalm**: a number alone means the whole psalm; add **From** and **To** for a part (Psalm 89, verses 31 to 47).
- The **verse of the day** is picked among the day's passages (the first by default). Its text is typed by hand, in Louis Segond 1910 (French) or World English Bible (English): without text, no verse is shown.
