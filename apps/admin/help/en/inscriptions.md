# Create a registration form

## What it is

A **registration form** lets people sign up for an activity (retreat, concert, catechism, outing) from the site. You see who is coming and who is waiting, you check people in on arrival, and the details are **deleted automatically 6 months after the activity**: the countdown is written in colour on each form.

## Step by step

1. Open **Registrations**, then **New form**.
2. **Form** tab: title, day of the activity, places, waitlist, and who can register (anyone, or members). For a children's activity, turn on **Activity for minors**: the parent or guardian registers and enters each child's first name and age band.
3. Add a few **questions** if needed (meal, t-shirt size). You cannot ask about health, origin, religion, politics or money.
4. Optional: enter the **linked event**'s address from the Calendar: a "Register" button appears on its page.
5. **Save**, then **Publish**.

## While people register

- **Registered** tab: numbers, WhatsApp, answers. When a place opens, the next person becomes "Confirmed" and you see **To tell**: call them, then click **Told**.
- **Add someone**: for a registration made by phone or on paper.
- **Check-in** tab: tap a name on arrival. No phone number is shown there.
- **Export (CSV)** is for the secretariat and the super admin only.

## Good to know

- After the set day, names and numbers are erased; only anonymous totals remain. Export before then if needed.
- At a person's request you can delete their registration right away (bin).
