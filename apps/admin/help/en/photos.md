# Add photos with good descriptions

## What it is

The **Media library** is the site’s photo store. Each photo is made lighter, stripped of its GPS position and described in one sentence before it is used in a news item, a page or an event.

## Step by step

1. Open **Médiathèque** (media library), then drop your photos (or **Ajouter des fichiers**).
2. Photos are lightened and the GPS position removed **on your device**, before upload.
3. For each photo, write a **description**: a sentence saying what is seen. It is read to blind and low-vision people and shown if the image fails to load.
4. For a photo where people can be recognised, **ask their agreement** before publishing.

## A good example

"Choir members in blue robes sing standing in front of the altar." Not: "IMG_2041".

Photos: 15 MB at most. Audio (MP3, M4A): 80 MB. PDF: 20 MB.
