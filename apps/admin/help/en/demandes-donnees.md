# Answer a data request

## What it is

Everyone has the right to **see** what the parish keeps about them, to **have it corrected**, to **have it erased** or to **object to its use**. A member does it themselves in "Mon espace" (a copy of their data, deleting the account). This screen is for people **without an account**, or who ask by phone, in person or in writing. It keeps the trace: who asked, what, when, the deadline and what was done.

## Step by step

1. **Log a request** as soon as it arrives: the kind, the name, how it came in and, if the person wishes, a number or address to answer them. The **30-day deadline** runs from the day it was received.
2. **Take charge** when you start.
3. Do what is asked (hand over a copy in person, correct, erase on the relevant screen).
4. **Close**: choose "Handled" or "Refused (legal reason)" and write in a few words what was done. **Never the person's data**: only "copy handed over in person".

## Deadlines

- **Overdue**: the last day has passed. **Soon**: under 5 days. People who may read this log get a notification.
- The 30-day deadline is the cautious reading of the law; it will be confirmed with the legal notes.

## Good to know

- A closed request is erased from the log 24 months after it was closed.
- Never delete an open request: close it, then let time do its work.
- When in doubt (a parent asking for a child, a third party, an authority), speak to the pastor or the Council before answering.
