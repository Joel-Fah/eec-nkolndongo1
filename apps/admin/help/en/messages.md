# Answer incoming messages

Contact form messages arrive in **Messages**.

1. Open **Messages**. New ones are marked.
2. Click a message to read it. Click **Prendre en charge** (take it) to say "I'm on it".
3. Reply to the person by WhatsApp, phone or e-mail, as they indicated.
4. Add an **internal note** if useful (visible to the team only), then **Marquer comme traité** (mark as handled).

## Prayer requests

They are **confidential**: only authorised people see them. Do not copy or forward them.

## Good to know

- Messages are deleted automatically after a delay (12 months after handling; 3 months for prayers).
- To delete one earlier, use **Supprimer** (permanent).
