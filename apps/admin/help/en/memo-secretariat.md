# Cheat sheet: Secretariat

## What it is

This one-page sheet is meant for printing: it sums up what the **secretariat** does (messages, members and outbox).

## Step by step

1. **Messages**: read → Prendre en charge → reply → Marquer comme traité.
2. **Boîte d'envoi**: copy the link → send it → Marquer comme transmis.
3. **Utilisateurs**: Inviter quelqu'un → role (and group) → send the link.
4. **Prayers**: confidential. Do not copy or forward.

Full help: **Aide** menu.
