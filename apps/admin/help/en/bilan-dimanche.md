# Enter the Sunday report

## What it is

The **Sunday report** keeps, for each Sunday, the **number of people** (men, women, children) and the **offerings** with their **purpose** (ordinary offering, tithe, works, Harvest festival, or a free purpose such as Reformation). These are **totals**: never a name, never a donor. **Nothing is public.** The figures are used to see **trends**, by week or by month.

## Step by step

1. Open **Sunday report**, then **Last Sunday** (or pick another date: it must be a Sunday).
2. **Secretariat**: in **Attendance**, enter men, women, children. For a particular Sunday, tick **Special Sunday** and name it.
3. **Treasurer**: in **Offerings**, choose the **purpose** (or "Other…" for a free one, suggested every time afterwards), the amount and, if the offering is for a group, the group. **With no group, it is the congregation.**
4. For a **Harvest** offering, choose the campaign: the amount is also offered for **validation** in the campaign and counts in the public total only once validated.
5. **Secretariat**: **Close this Sunday** when everything is checked. It can be reopened.

## Trends

**Trends** tab: attendance and offerings by month or by week, with the change from the previous period, the total by purpose and by group. **Export (CSV)** is for the secretariat and the super admin only.

## Good to know

- A group leader only sees **their** group's offerings, and not the attendance.
- The internal note must not name anyone.
- Rights are given in **Roles** (secretariat: read, attendance, close, export; treasurer: read, offerings).
- A day that is not a Sunday (Christmas, Good Friday…) is entered like a Sunday: pick its date in “Another day”, then **name it** before entering anything.
- The **bell** at the top reminds you of the last four weeks' Sundays still missing attendance or offerings; a click opens the right Sunday, and the reminder ticks itself once it is entered.
- An **editor** can enter the figures right after the service: they are **proposals** (“To validate”). The secretariat validates attendance, the treasurer the offerings; until then nothing counts in the totals, and the Sunday cannot be closed.
