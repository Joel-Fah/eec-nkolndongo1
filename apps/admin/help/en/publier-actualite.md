# Publish a news item

## What it is

A **news item** is a text that appears on the site’s **News** page: an announcement, a report, an invitation. Until it is published, nobody sees it.

## Step by step

1. Open **Actualités** in the menu, then **Écrire une actualité** (write a news item).
2. Write the **title** and the **text**. The summary is optional: without one, the start of the text is used.
3. Add a photo if you have one (see "Add photos").
4. English is optional. If you leave it empty, English-speaking visitors see the French version with a note.
5. Click **Publier**. The item shows on the site within about two minutes.

> Your work is saved automatically. Until you publish, nobody sees it.

## If you cannot publish

A group leader writes, the secretariat reviews and publishes.

1. Write the news post (or the event): the title and the text are required.
2. In the **Status** panel, click **Ask for publication**. The people who may publish are notified (the bell).
3. The panel says « Publication requested on… ». You can keep editing: the secretariat sees the latest version.
4. When it is published, the request closes by itself.

The same goes for Calendar events.

## Good to know

- **Preview**: a secret link, valid for 3 days, to show the draft to someone without an account.
- **Retirer du site** (take off the site): the item goes back to a draft.
- A group leader can only write for their own group.
