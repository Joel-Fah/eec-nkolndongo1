# Follow my group

## What it is

"My group" is for a **group's leaders** (and the secretariat): you see the people of **your** group, the requests to join it and a few figures. To create or configure the groups themselves (name, public page, leaders), use the **Groups** screen. You never see a group other than your own.

## Step by step

1. If you have several groups, pick one at the top.
2. **Requests to join**: **Accept** or **Decline**. You can write a short message the person sees with the answer.
3. **Members**: one card per person, with their avatar, role and joining date. The **Call** and **WhatsApp** buttons only show if the person chose to share their number with the group.
4. **Remove** someone: a reason is required. It stays in the activity log; neither the person nor the public sees it.
5. **Announcements** opens the group's announcements screen.

## The figures

- **Members** and **joined this month**.
- **Ages**: shown only from 5 people whose age is known, so nobody can be singled out.

## Good to know

- The **CSV export** is for people who hold that right: it is limited to your group and every export is noted in the log.
- Belonging to a group never makes anyone's name public.
- Requests left unanswered are erased after 6 months.
