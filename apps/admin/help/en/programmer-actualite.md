# Schedule a news item

## What it is

**Scheduling** a news item means writing it now and choosing the day and time when it appears on its own on the site.

## Step by step

1. Open the news item (or create one).
2. Click **Publier**. Under **Quand ?** (when), choose **Plus tard, à un jour et une heure choisis** (later) and enter the date and time.
3. Click **Programmer** (schedule). The state becomes **Programmée** and the date is shown ("Sera visible le …").

## Good to know

- You can also fill **Retirer du site le** (optional): the item leaves the site by itself.
- To change the date, open the item and click **Dates**.
- The time is Yaoundé time. The item appears within two minutes after the chosen time.
