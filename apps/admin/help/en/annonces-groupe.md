# Write a group announcement

## What it is

An **announcement** is a short message for a group's members: a moved meeting, a reminder, good news. Once published, each member gets it in "Mon espace" (Groups tab) and as a **notification**. There is no e-mail or SMS. Only the group's members read it.

## Step by step

1. Choose the **group** (you only see yours).
2. **New announcement**: a title and a message in French; English is optional (otherwise French is shown).
3. Optional: a **link** (a page of the site like `/en/calendar/`, or an https address) and a **last day shown**.
4. **Save**: the announcement stays a **draft**, nobody sees it.
5. **Publish**: members get the notification. Publishing twice does not send two notifications.
6. **Take down** when it is over: it disappears from Mon espace and its notifications are closed.

## Good to know

- The message is short (1,000 characters at most): better to link to the event or the form.
- A member can silence announcement notifications in Mon espace; the announcement stays visible in Groups.
- A taken-down announcement is erased 12 months later.
