# What does "validate" mean?

## What it is

**Validating** means confirming that a piece of information (time, address, giving number, figure) is correct. On the site, **facts** only appear once an authorised person has checked and validated them; until then the site says “soon” or shows nothing.

## If you validate

1. Open **À valider** (to validate) in the menu.
2. For each line, compare with reality (call, check the official paper).
3. If it is right: **Valider**. Otherwise: **Demander une correction** and write what is wrong (and the right information if you know it).

## If you are asked for a correction

Fix the value on its screen, then mark the request as **handled**. The validator will check it again.

> When in doubt, do not validate. "Soon" is better than wrong information.
