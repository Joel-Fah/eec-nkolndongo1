# Update service times

## What it is

The **times** are the list of services and meetings of the week, as visitors see them on the site. They are managed in **Services and times**.

## Step by step

1. Open **Cultes et horaires** and pick the day.
2. Click a row to edit it, or **Ajouter** (add) for a new one.
3. Save.

> A time is **a fact**: it only shows on the site once it is **approved** by an authorised person. Any edit removes the approval until it is reviewed again.

For a public holiday or a special service, create an event in the Agenda instead.
