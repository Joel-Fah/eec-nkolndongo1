# Compose the Sunday bulletin

1. Open **Bulletin**. Pick the **Sunday** (the coming one is suggested) and how to start: **Fill from the site** (this Sunday, the agenda, the meditation, the news), **Copy the previous bulletin**, or **Start empty**. Click **Composer** (compose).
2. Check each **section**: change the titles and lines, add or remove lines, move a section up or down. English is optional.
3. For a word of the week, add a **Free text** section.
4. Optional: paste the address of the **paper bulletin's PDF** (for example on Google Drive).
5. Click **Publier** (publish). The page is on the site, to print in **A4** or **A5** or save as a PDF.

## Good to know

- **Fill from the site** replaces the automatic sections; your free-text sections stay.
- It only takes what is **already public**: a Sunday not published in “Liturgie” will not appear.
- Your work is saved automatically. Until the bulletin is published, nobody sees it.
