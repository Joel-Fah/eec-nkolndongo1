# Compose the Sunday bulletin

## What it is

The **bulletin** is the week’s sheet, one per Sunday: what happens this Sunday, the week’s agenda, each day’s meditation and the announcements. It appears on the site, where anyone can print it or save it as a PDF (the paper size is chosen in the print window). It is not retyped: its sections come from the other screens (Liturgy, Events, Meditations, News); you tick what you want in, and you can add a free text.

## Step by step

1. Open **Bulletin**. Pick the **Sunday** (the coming one is suggested) and how to start: **Fill from the site**, **Copy the previous bulletin**, or **Start empty**. Click **Compose**.
2. Go through the **This Sunday**, **The week ahead**, **Meditations** and **Announcements** sections. They come from the site: **tick** the lines to put in the bulletin. Nothing is retyped here; to change a text, fix it on its own screen (Liturgy, Events, Meditations, News) then click **Update from the site**.
3. An empty section says where the information should come from and offers to open that screen.
4. For a word of the week, click **Add a free text**: it is the only part written here. English is optional.
5. Optional: paste the address of the **paper bulletin's PDF** (for example on Google Drive).
6. Click **Publish**. The page is on the site, to print or save as a PDF.

## Good to know

- **The agenda covers the bulletin's Sunday and the six days after it** (Sunday to Saturday), not only the Sunday.
- The bulletin only takes what is **already public**: a Sunday not published in “Liturgy” will not appear.
- Your work is saved automatically. Until the bulletin is published, nobody sees it.
