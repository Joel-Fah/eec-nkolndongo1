# Manage the Communion cards

## What it is

Holy Supper is on the **second Sunday of each month**; the **preparation week** is the one before (Monday to Saturday). This page manages the communicants' digital card: who has one, who had it signed this month and what was received. Members see their card in "Mon espace"; they only see what is **expected** as a contribution, never a debt, and nothing is refused if it is not paid.

## The tabs

- **Cards**: the month's work. One line per card: status, month's visa, contribution received, actions. For the Elders, the secretariat, the pastor and the treasurer.
- **New card**: confirm a baptism, then propose a card. For the secretariat and the pastor. The list already shows the members who declared their baptism and wait for your confirmation; search (name, e-mail, phone) finds the others.
- **Expected contributions**: one amount per age band and per group. Edited by the treasurer, read by the others. Leave empty to expect nothing.
- **Check a card**: the QR scanner, for the Holy Supper entrance.

## A card's statuses

- **To approve**: proposed, not valid yet. A different person than the one who proposed it must approve. The member does not see it yet.
- **Valid**: the member sees their card and QR code. The month can be signed and contributions noted.
- **Suspended**: the card stops working (the QR says "not valid"). The member sees "see the pastor", never the reason.
- **Expired**: the validity date has passed; renew it.
- **Withdrawn**: removed by the pastor. The reason stays in the activity log.

## The actions

- **Confirm baptism**: you checked the baptism register. No card without it.
- **Propose a card**: valid until the end of the year, it stays "to approve".
- **Approve**: the second person makes the card valid. You cannot approve your own proposal.
- **Sign the month**: the Elders' signature for the chosen month's Holy Supper. The member's streak grows. **Remove the visa** fixes a mistake.
- **Note a contribution**: the amount received this month, in the register (Elders, secretariat, treasurer).
- **Suspend / Restore**: pauses the card then puts it back (secretariat).
- **Renew**: extends the card (pastor). The old QR code stops working.
- **Withdraw**: removes the card (pastor), with a reason kept in the log.

## Common situations

**A new communicant**

1. The member declares their baptism in their profile.
2. **New card**: they appear in "Baptisms to confirm". Check the register, then **Confirm baptism**.
3. **Propose a card**.
4. The pastor opens **Cards**, finds "To approve" and clicks **Approve**.

**Holy Supper Sunday (Elders)**

1. **Cards**: choose the current month.
2. For each person who took communion: **Sign the month**.
3. Note the contribution received, if given.
4. A member shown "Excused" told you themselves: their streak is paused, nothing to do.

**A new year**: filter "Expired", then **More › Renew** and choose the new date.

**Checking at the entrance**: **Check a card › Open the camera** and aim at the QR code (or paste the address). "Valid card" or "not valid" shows, with the first name.

**Someone should no longer take communion**: for a pause, **More › Suspend** then **Restore** later. For a definitive case, the pastor chooses **More › Withdraw**, with a reason.

## Good to know

- A proposed card becomes valid only when another person approves it.
- Baptism is sensitive data: only the people who issue cards see it.
- Everything is erased with the member's account.
