# Edit a group page

## What it is

A **group page** (choir, youth, women…) is the group’s public introduction on the site: a tagline, meeting days, a text and a way to contact them. Each group has its own; its leader edits it.

## Step by step

1. Open **Groupes**, pick the group, then **Page publique**.
2. Fill in the tagline, the meeting days, the presentation text and a way to get in touch.
3. To show a person (name or photo) you need **their agreement**, which you tick in their record. Without it, only their role appears.
4. Publish the page.

## Good to know

- A group leader only edits **their own** group.
- You can remove a person at any time: they disappear from the site within a few minutes.
