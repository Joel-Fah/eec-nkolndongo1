# Cheat sheet: Group leader

## What it is

This one-page sheet is meant for printing: it sums up what a **group leader** (the person who manages their group’s page and people) does.

## Step by step

1. **Group page**: Groupes → your group → Page publique → Publier.
2. **People shown**: only with their agreement (tick box).
3. **News and events** for your group: Actualités / Agenda.
4. **Need something else?** Ask the secretariat.

Full help: **Aide** menu.
