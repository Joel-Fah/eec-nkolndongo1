# 0047 — Member profile details, a private photo, and the virtual member card
**Status:** Accepted · 2026-10-25 (Joël: details wanted — birth date, gender, neighbourhood and city, marital status, profession; photo private by default with levels; a member card that cannot be downloaded yet)
**Context:** The parish wants to know and understand its congregation (age bands, where people live, what they do) and to give each member a recognisable space. Staff accounts are members too. This is **new personal data**, so its limits are written before any code (Loi n°2024/017, `docs/legal/data-protection.md`).
**Decision:**
1. **Sign-up and sign-in stay exactly as they are.** Once signed in, « Mon espace » **suggests** completing the profile. It never blocks anything.
2. **Details, all optional, each with its purpose shown next to the field, never pre-filled:** birth date (to plan for age groups; staff see an **age band**, never the exact date), gender (statistics only), neighbourhood + city (to know where the congregation lives; **no street address, no GPS**), marital status and profession (to understand the congregation's life and skills). **Nothing else**: no ethnic origin, region of origin, political or religious-sensitive data, no health data (data-protection.md « out of scope »).
3. **Consent kind `profile`**: explicit, unticked, withdrawable at any time, with the policy version. **Withdrawing erases the details** (not the account). The privacy policy version is bumped (0033) and members are told.
4. **Statistics are aggregates** in the admin (counts by age band, by city); small groups (fewer than 5 people) are not shown separately. The CSV export carries the new columns only for the existing dangerous export permission and is audited by row count.
5. **Photo: private by default.** Levels: **only me** (default) or **parish staff who manage members**. It is **never public on the site** (a rule, not an option). Stored as one 512 px WebP with metadata removed in the browser, under a key prefix **outside** the public media keys, served **only** by a session-checked route (`private, no-store`, never edge-cached, never in the offline copy). Deleting the photo, withdrawing consent or deleting the account deletes the object.
6. **Without a photo, or while it is private, everyone sees a generated abstract avatar** (deterministic from the account id, brand colours, GitHub-style) in the public header, « Mon espace » and the admin top bar.
7. **Member card:** virtual, shown in « Mon espace » with a gentle tilt (off under reduced motion, static on touch). Name, a short member code derived from the account id (no sequence that leaks how many members exist), the year the account was opened, the parish. **No download, share or print for now.**
8. **Warnings:** an incomplete profile shows a checklist in « Mon espace » and a warning badge on the avatar in the public header and in the admin top bar. Warn only.
9. **Retention:** with the account; included in « my data » (JSON copy) and removed with the account after the 30-day grace period.
**Consequences:** migration 0034 (additive: columns on `profiles`, consent kind `profile`); API on `/api/me`; `modules/members` shows age band and place to staff with `members:read`, the photo only at the *staff* level; `docs/legal/data-protection.md` and `modules/members/README.md` updated; decision 0031's pattern (who decides what leaves, audited) applies. **Production:** migration 0034 and the policy-version notice are Joël's steps.
**Not now:** a downloadable or shareable card, a public member directory, any use of the details for decisions about a person.

**Amended 2026-10-25 (Joël's review):**
- **A Google photo counts.** People who sign in with Google already have the picture Google gave; it is their photo until they choose another (the completeness card no longer asks for one). They can keep it, upload a different one, or remove it, and the visibility level (only me / staff who manage members) applies to either. The Google picture is not copied into our storage; a picture that cannot load gives way to the generated avatar.
- **The generated avatar is our own drawing**, not a GitHub-style grid: a soft tint of one of our tones, translucent circles, a ring, a dotted arc and a low wave. It is square and fills its frame (rounded square in the public header, circle in the admin bar), never a circle inside a circle.
- **The warning mark** uses the warning background, not red.
- **The member card is the communion card.** The « Carte de Sainte-Cène » that was announced and this member card are one object: the card built here will carry the communion function when that phase comes. The separate « soon » tile is removed.
