# Sunday to-do: what to collect on site every week

A checklist for the person on site. Collect it on paper or in your phone's notes, then enter it in the admin afterwards.
Nothing here needs to be typed live. Admin screens are named in brackets.

**Never write a person's name next to a number** (attendance, offerings). Counts and amounts only.

---

## A. During or right after the service: the Bilan (needs to be entered every week)

Screen: **Bilan du dimanche** (`/admin/m/sundays`). Enter by the following Monday: the app reminds you.

### 1. Attendance (secretariat)
For **each service** of the day (name it, e.g. "Culte 1", "Culte 2", "Culte des enfants"):
- [ ] Number of **men**
- [ ] Number of **women**
- [ ] Number of **children**
- [ ] Short internal note if something is worth remembering (max 120 characters, **no names**)

### 2. Offerings (treasurer)
One line per **purpose**:
- [ ] **Purpose**: ordinaire, dîme, travaux, Moissons, reconnaissance, other (write it)
- [ ] **Amount in XAF**
- [ ] **Group**, only if the offering is for a specific group (otherwise it is the congregation's)
- [ ] **Campaign**, only for Moissons (the line then feeds the campaign total once the secretariat validates it)
- [ ] **Never the donor's name**

### 3. Special days
If the day is **not** an ordinary Sunday (Christmas, Easter, a vigil, a weekday service):
- [ ] The **name of the day** (required) and the date

Then the secretariat **closes** the Sunday once everything is entered.

---

## B. Before the service: what the site shows for "Ce dimanche"

Screen: **Liturgie** (`/admin/m/liturgy`). Ideally collected by Thursday for the coming Sunday.

- [ ] **Name of the Sunday** (FR, and EN if you have it)
- [ ] **Theme / message** of the day (FR, EN)
- [ ] **Bible readings** (book, chapter, verses)
- [ ] **Hymns** (numbers or titles)
- [ ] **Holy Supper** this Sunday? (yes/no). It is normally the second Sunday of the month.
- [ ] **Preacher** and **liturgist** names: **only if they agreed to be shown** (ask first, tick the consent)
- [ ] **Notes for the congregation** (announcements that belong on the page)

---

## C. After the service: content to publish

| What | Screen | What to collect |
|---|---|---|
| **Sermon** | Sermons | Title (FR/EN), preacher, passage, the date, the **audio** file or the **YouTube link**. Series name if it belongs to one. |
| **Bulletin** | Bulletin | The **PDF** of the week, a title (FR/EN) |
| **Photos** | Médiathèque | Photos of the day. **Only people who agreed to be shown.** Add a short description (FR and EN) |
| **News** | Actualités | Anything that happened and deserves a post: title, 2-3 sentences, a photo |
| **Announcements** (for groups) | Annonces de groupe | Message for the members of a group |

---

## D. Things to note and bring to the secretariat

- [ ] **Registrations on site**: people who signed up on paper or by phone for an upcoming event (name, phone, number of people, which event). Enter them in **Inscriptions**.
- [ ] **Contact requests** made in person (prayer, visit, question): give them to the secretariat to enter in **Messages**.
- [ ] **Baptisms or confirmations** to record, **only if the person agreed**: the secretariat confirms them in the Communion cards screen.
- [ ] **Holy Supper Sunday only**: the Elders' **visa** for the month and the **contributions** received, in **Cartes de Sainte-Cène** (a contribution is recorded as "received", never as "owed").
- [ ] **Changes to times, address or contacts** you notice (they must be validated before they go public).

---

## E. Weekly rhythm (suggested)

| When | What |
|---|---|
| **Thursday** | Collect B (Sunday programme) and enter it |
| **Sunday** | Collect A (counts and offerings) on paper, then C (sermon, bulletin, photos) and D |
| **Monday** | Enter A and close the Sunday. The app sends a reminder if it is late |
| **Monday to Wednesday** | Publish C |

## Notes
- Figures you enter count nowhere until another person validates them. You cannot validate your own entry.
- Everything in A is **internal**: money and counts are never shown on the public site.
- If a field has no answer yet, leave it empty rather than guessing. Do not invent names, dates or times.
