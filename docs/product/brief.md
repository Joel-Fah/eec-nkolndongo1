# Product brief

## Why
Give the Paroisse EEC Nkolndongo 1 a home online that people *want* to come back to — and give the
parish staff a simple, trustworthy tool to run day-to-day communication and administration.

## Who
| Audience | Needs |
|---|---|
| Parishioners (local) | Times, news, agenda, sermons, bulletin, how-to for sacraments, giving |
| Diaspora | Stay connected, listen to sermons, give remotely |
| Visitors / seekers | Discover who we are, feel welcome, find us |
| Members (logged in) | Be counted, later: member services (carte de Sainte-Cène, inscriptions…) |
| Staff (secretariat, editors, department leads, treasurer) | Publish and manage without IT skills |
| Super admin (Joël) | Define roles, groups, permissions, settings, modules |

## Principles
1. **Function first.** Every screen answers a real need of one of the audiences above.
2. **An experience per page.** Each page tells its part of the story differently (see `pages.md`).
3. **Light on data.** Built for mid-range Android on 3G/4G.
4. **Trust & hope.** Warm, calm, honest tone; accurate information; respectful of privacy.
5. **French first, bilingual everywhere** (public + admin). **Light and dark** themes.
6. **Built to grow.** New features plug in as modules.

## Success signals (first year)
- Weekly content actually published by staff without developer help.
- Members registering (headcount visible in admin).
- Lighthouse mobile ≥ 90 performance / 100 accessibility on key pages.
- Fewer "what time is…?" questions to the secretariat.

## Out of scope for now
Domain purchase · online payments integration · sensitive pastoral registers · native apps.
