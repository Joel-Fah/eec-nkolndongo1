# Registre des traitements (Phase 05 : entrées ajoutées ; le reste à compléter au Checkpoint L)

> **À VALIDER.** Ce registre est complété au fil des phases. Les bases légales et les durées marquées « à confirmer » attendent les notes juridiques (Checkpoint L, reporté). Lecture prudente appliquée en attendant.

| Traitement | Données | Finalité | Qui voit | Conservation | Lieu | Base / remarque |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Demande pour rejoindre un groupe | personne, groupe, case « partager mon numéro », message de réponse, dates | décider qui entre dans un groupe | responsables du groupe, secrétariat | décidée : 12 mois après la réponse ; en attente : 6 mois ; avec le compte | Neon (UE à confirmer) | consentement (case non cochée), à confirmer |
| Appartenance à un groupe | personne, groupe, titre, partage du numéro | vie du groupe | responsables du groupe (leur groupe), secrétariat | avec le compte | idem | consentement ; jamais public |
| Annonces de groupe | titre, texte, lien, dates, auteur | informer les membres | membres du groupe, responsables | expirée : 12 mois après | idem | intérêt légitime / mission |
| Carte de Sainte-Cène | carte, statut, version, validité, qui a proposé / approuvé | attester la communion | membre (sa carte), émetteurs, Anciens, trésorier | avec le compte | idem | **donnée sensible** : accord explicite, accès restreint |
| Visas, excuses | mois visés, mois excusés (sans motif) | suivre la Sainte-Cène | Anciens, secrétariat, pasteur ; le membre voit les siens | avec le compte | idem | idem |
| Cotisations (registre) | montant reçu par mois | tenue du registre des Anciens | Anciens, secrétariat, trésorier | avec le compte | idem | le membre ne voit que le montant attendu |
| Déclaration de baptême / confirmation | oui / non déclaré ; confirmation de la paroisse (qui, quand) | préparer la carte | secrétariat, pasteur | avec le compte (la déclaration part avec le retrait du profil complet) | idem | **donnée sensible** : accord explicite (fenêtre d'explication) |
| Copie de la carte sur l'appareil | la dernière carte (IndexedDB) | l'ouvrir sans réseau | le membre, sur son appareil | 45 jours, effacée à la déconnexion | navigateur | — |
| Registre des demandes sur les données | type, nom, moyen de répondre, dates, issue | répondre à temps aux droits des personnes | secrétariat | clôturée : 24 mois après (à confirmer) | idem | obligation légale, à confirmer |
| Notifications de membre | type, titre court, lien | informer | le membre | avec le compte | idem | — |

## Par module (généré depuis les README des modules)

| Module | Données gardées | Pourquoi | Exposition | Conservation | Responsables |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Module `announcements` — Annonces de groupe | `announcements_items` (group, French and optional English title and text, optional link, state, publish and expiry dates, author). The notifications it raises live in `notifications` (one per member and announcement, removed with the person). | Group: who may read it. Text: what to tell. Dates: when it appears and stops appearing. Author: accountability (audit). | Only the members of the group (their Mon espace) and the group's leaders / the secretariat. Nothing is public. A group's member list is never part of an announcement. | An expired announcement is erased 12 months after it expired (daily job, `apps/api/src/retention.ts`); a deleted group takes its announcements with it. | Group leaders (their group), secretariat (all). |
| Module `bulletin` — Bulletin du dimanche | `bulletins` (week, state, title, blocks, PDF address, who wrote it). The blocks come from content that is already public; it may name people who agreed to appear. | The week and blocks: to compose and print. Author: accountability. | Admin: `bulletin:edit\|publish`. Public: the published bulletin. | Kept (public content). | Secrétariat, éditeur. |
| Module `campaigns` — Campagnes (Fête des Moissons) | `campaigns` (title, dates, goal), `campaign_progress` (amount, date received, an optional group, a short note, validation), `campaign_group_goals`. **No donor name, no link to a member.** The internal note must not name anyone. | Amounts and dates: to follow the campaign. Group: totals per department. Validation: a figure counts publicly only once a second person validated it. | Public: the validated total (and per-group totals) only. Admin: `campaigns:read\|edit\|progress\|validate`; the treasurer enters but cannot validate, the secretariat validates but cannot enter. | Kept with the campaign (aggregates, no personal data). | Trésorier (entry), secrétariat (definition and validation). |
| Module `communion` — Cartes de Sainte-Cène | `communion_cards` (person, status, version, last day of validity, who proposed and approved), `communion_visas` (card, month, who signed), `communion_excuses` (card, month: the member's word, no reason), `communion_fees` (expected amount by age band or group), `communion_contributions` (card, month, amount received, who noted it). On `profiles`: the member's own declaration (baptised, confirmed: only with the `profile` consent) and who confirmed the baptism from the register and when. Religious-belief data. | The card and its version: what is valid, and an old QR code stops working when a card is renewed. Visa and excuse: the monthly Holy Supper and the streak. Fees and contributions: the Elders' register; the member only sees « expected », never a balance. Sacrament confirmation: a card is for someone whose baptism the parish confirmed. | The member sees their own card. The Elders, the secretariat, the pasteur and the treasurer see the cards (not the profile). The contributions register: the Elders, the secretariat and the treasurer. The public check shows a first name and an initial and nothing else, no account needed, rate-limited, never cached. | Visas, excuses, contributions and the card (even a withdrawn one) are kept while the account exists and erased with it (30 days after a deletion request, by cascade; the daily job in `apps/api/src/retention.ts` purges the account). Nothing is kept longer than the account (decision 0050). | Secretariat and pasteur (cards), Anciens d'Église (visas, register), treasurer (fees). |
| Module `contact` — Messages | `contact_messages` (name, email or phone, category, message, optional subject, status, assignee, the time the visitor ticked the consent box) and `contact_notes`. **No IP address is stored**: only a salted hash is used, as a rate-limit key that expires within a day. | — | — | (daily job, `apps/api/src/retention.ts`): 12 months after « handled » (at most 24 months after receipt); prayer requests 3 months after « handled » (at most 6 months after receipt). | — |
| Module `departments` — Départements | `department_pages` (1:1 with a group), `department_people` (name, role FR/EN, optional photo, **public consent** with who recorded it and when), `department_quiz` (questions and answer→tag mapping). The quiz stores **nothing about visitors**: it runs in the browser. | — | Only published pages (`/api/departments/public*`). A person's **name and photo are sent only when consent is recorded**; otherwise only their role. Withdrawing consent removes the name from the next cache refresh (a few seconds). A group's approved schedule slots and latest news/events are read by the page from their own modules. | Kept until deleted; removing a person deletes their row. Consent changes appear in the audit trail. | — |
| Module `events` — Agenda | Table `events_items` (titles, descriptions, start/end, time zone, recurrence and exceptions as JSON, place text, optional https registration link, cover, group); shared revisions. **No personal data about parishioners.** | — | Cached JSON `/api/events/public*`, pages under `/fr/agenda/evenements/`, calendar feeds `/fr/agenda/calendrier.ics[?groupe=key]`, `.ics` per event. | Kept until archived or deleted. | — |
| Module `guides` — Vie pratique | `guides`, `guide_steps`. **No personal data.** The visitor's ticks stay in their own browser (`localStorage`), never on the server. | — | Only **published** guides (`/api/guides/public*`). The structure is seeded as drafts; the wording stays `[À VALIDER]` until the content partner validates it, so nothing unvalidated shows in production. | Kept until deleted. | — |
| Module `legal` — Documents légaux | `legal_documents` (document key, version, label, FR/EN bodies, change summary, purpose-changed flag, who published and when). **No personal data.** | — | — | Every published version is kept (the history is the record of what people agreed to). | — |
| Module `liturgy` — Liturgie | `liturgy_sundays` (including preacher and liturgist **display names**, public only when the consent box is ticked; who confirmed and when are recorded), `liturgy_seasons` (approved before public), `liturgy_verses`. Names are never copied to another week. | — | `/api/liturgy/public/{sunday,sundays,seasons,verse}` (cached); pages `/fr/agenda/ce-dimanche`, the dial on `/fr/agenda/annee-liturgique`, the home card and verse. | Kept until deleted by an authorised person. | — |
| Module `media` — Médiathèque | Table `media_files` (name, kind, sizes, variants, placeholder, FR/EN descriptions, captions, credit, source) and `media_usages` (where a file is used, so a file in use cannot be deleted). Files live in the S3-compatible store (Neon Object Storage today, R2 later: four variables and a copy, decision 0020). **No personal data**, unless someone uploads a photo of people: only with consent (`docs/legal/`). | — | `GET /media/<kind>/<yyyy>/<mm>/<id>/<variant>.<ext>`: immutable, Range, ETag, nosniff, no listing. The bucket is never public. Astro `<Picture>` renders the variants. | Kept until archived or deleted by an authorised person. | Secrétariat (upload, descriptions), super admin (delete). |
| Module `meditations` — Méditations | `meditation_plans`, `meditation_days` (date, headline, readings, psalm, reflection, verse), `bible_verses` (translation, book, chapter, verse, text). **No personal data.** | To publish the daily meditation and passages. | Public once published. Admin: `meditations:read\|edit\|publish`. | Kept (public content). | Éditeur. |
| Module `members` — Membres | Nothing of its own: it reads `user`, `profiles` (first and last name, optional phone, language, status, and, if the member chose to add them, birth date, gender, neighbourhood and city, marital status, profession and a private photo: decision 0047), `consents` (privacy, news and profile, with policy version and dates) and `group_members`. | Name and email: to know who has an account. Phone: optional, only if the person gave it. Groups: what the person says they take part in. Birth date, gender, place, marital status, profession: to understand the congregation (age bands, where people live, skills), shown to staff as an age band and aggregates. Photo: recognition, private by default. Consents: proof of agreement (Loi n°2024/017). | Admin only. `members:read` (list, records, aggregate numbers), `members:export` (sensitive: CSV with names, emails and phones; every export is audited). The dashboard numbers are aggregates, with no names. | The account is kept while active. After a deletion request it is purged after 30 days by the daily retention job (`apps/api/src/retention.ts`). Audit entries are kept 24 months. | Secrétariat (read), super admin (export). |
| Module `news` — Actualités | Table `news_posts` (titles, summaries, rich-text bodies as JSON, cover, slug, dates, group); shared `content_revisions` and `content_redirects`; `media_usages` rows for photos used. **No personal data about parishioners.** Staff ids appear only as `created_by` / `updated_by` and in the audit log. | — | Cached JSON at `/api/news/public*`, pages under `/fr/actualites/nouvelles/` (`/en/news/latest/`), RSS at `/api/news/public/feed.xml`. Only what is live now (published, start passed, end not reached). | Kept until archived or deleted; revisions are limited to the last 50 per post. | — |
| Module `registrations` — Inscriptions | `registration_forms` (title, event day, capacity, audience, optional questions), `registrations` (code, status, name, phone, WhatsApp, optional e-mail, party size, answers, guardian confirmation, the time of consent and the policy version, the account if signed in, check-in and notification times) and `registration_people` (a child's first name and age band). `registration_counts`: anonymous totals per form, kept after the people are erased. | Name and phone: to reach the person (a place freed, a change). Party size and answers: to organise the activity. Child's first name and age band: for activities for minors, with a parent's confirmation. Consent time and version: proof of agreement. No question may ask about health, origin, religion, politics or money. | Admin: `registrations:read\|edit\|manage` (group-scoped); the CSV export (`registrations:export`) is for the super admin and the secretariat and is audited. The check-in list shows no phone number. A member sees only their own. | The people (and children, by cascade) are erased 6 months after the event, at most 12 months after the form closed (daily job, `apps/api/src/retention.ts`); the anonymous totals stay. | Secrétariat; group leaders for their group's forms. |
| Module `rights` — Demandes sur les données | `rights_requests`: kind, the person's name, a way to answer (optional), the channel, received on, due on, status, a few words on what was done, closed on, who handled it. | To answer on time and to be able to show that we did. The outcome is a few words, never the personal data itself. | Staff only: `rights:read`, `rights:handle`, `rights:delete` (dangerous). The activity log records that a request was noted or changed and its kind, never who. | Closed requests are erased 24 months after they were closed (daily job, `apps/api/src/retention.ts`; duration to confirm with the legal notes). | Secrétariat. |
| Module `schedule` — Cultes et horaires | `schedule_slots` (label, weekday, times, place, optional group, **validated** flag with who approved and when) and `schedule_exceptions`. **No personal data.** | — | Only **approved** slots (`/api/schedule/public*`); with none approved the page keeps its « bientôt » text. Editing an approved slot takes its approval away unless the editor may approve. | Kept until deleted; exceptions in the past are ignored by the public API. | — |
| Module `sermons` — Prédications | `sermon_series`, `sermons` (title, summary, text, transcript, date, series, the preacher's name and their **recorded consent**, passages, YouTube id, links, publishing dates). | Preacher's name: attribution, shown publicly only with a recorded consent (who, when) (decision 0031). The rest is public content. | Public once published. Admin: `sermons:read\|edit\|publish` (group-scoped). | Kept (public content). A preacher can withdraw consent: the name is then removed. | Éditeur, secrétariat. |
| Module `settings` — Paramètres | Table `settings`: one row per section (`parish`, `season`), each field `{ value, validated }`. No personal data about individuals: these are the parish's public contact details. Seeds are research-draft candidates, all **unvalidated** (`docs/content/README.md`); nothing invented. | — | Only approved values (`validated: true`) leave the API, through `/api/settings/public` (edge-cached, decision 0021). Editing a value without `settings:validate` removes its approval. | Kept while relevant; every change is in the audit log (24 months). | Secrétariat (edits), super admin (approval, flags). |
| Module `story` — Pages du site | `page_slots`, `history_milestones`, `council_members` (name, role, optional photo and term, **public consent** with who recorded it and when). | — | `/api/story/public`: published slots and milestones only; council **names and photos only with consent** (otherwise the role alone). An unpublished slot never replaces the built-in text. | Kept until deleted; removing a council member deletes their row; consent changes are in the audit trail. | — |
| Module `sundays` — Bilan du dimanche | `sunday_reports` (date, service, men / women / children counts, kind, an internal note, who entered and when, closing) and `sunday_offerings` (purpose, amount, optional group or campaign). **No name, no donor, no per-person attendance.** | Counts and amounts: to understand the life of the parish and report to the Conseil. Who entered and when: accountability. | Admin only: `sundays:read\|attendance\|offerings\|propose\|close\|export` (group leaders see only their group's lines). The CSV export is for the super admin and the secretariat. | Kept (aggregates, no personal data). | Secrétariat (attendance, closing), trésorier (offerings). |
