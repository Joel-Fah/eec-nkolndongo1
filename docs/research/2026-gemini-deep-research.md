Features must include unmetered bandwidth, robust SSL certificates for secure financial transactions, and server-side caching26. Given local network conditions, developers must implement aggressive image compression (converting formats to WebP) and utilize Content Delivery Networks (CDNs) to reduce server response times for users accessing the site via 3G/4G cellular networks29.

### **Sermon Archiving and Podcasting Infrastructure**

The digital archiving and distribution of weekly sermons is a primary pastoral function of the website.

* **Historical Vulnerability:** Developers must explicitly avoid installing *Sermon Manager*, which was historically the industry standard for WordPress church sites. * **Current Infrastructure:** The recommended alternatives are *Advanced Sermons* or *Church Content*30. These robust tools allow administrators to seamlessly categorize audio and video files by granular taxonomies: series, speaker name (e.g., Révérend Pasteur), topic, and specific biblical book30. Crucially, they automatically generate compliant podcast RSS feeds, allowing congregants locally and in the diaspora to subscribe to EEC Nkolndongo I sermons directly via Apple Podcasts, Spotify, or Google Podcasts30.

## **Legal Compliance and Data Protection Framework**

The regulatory and legal landscape regarding digital data in Cameroon underwent a historic paradigm shift with the promulgation of **Loi n°2024/017 du 23 décembre 2024 relative à la protection des données à caractère personnel au Cameroun**45. This legislation, inspired heavily by the European GDPR and the African Union's Malabo Convention, establishes strict operational mandates for how all institutions—expressly including religious organizations—handle personal data45.  
Because the church routinely collects highly sensitive personal data—such as baptismal records, financial contributions, marital counseling intakes, and membership registers—it is entirely subject to strict regulatory oversight. Compliance is legally mandatory by **June 23, 2026** (an 18-month grace period from the law's promulgation)45. Non-compliance can result in administrative injunctions, suspension of digital activities, reputational damage, and severe financial penalties reaching up to 1 billion FCFA, alongside potential criminal imprisonment (ranging from 6 months to 2 years) for gross negligence or data leaks48.

### **Key Legal Obligations for EEC Nkolndongo I**

> 1. **Lawfulness and Consent (Article 5):** All data collection mechanisms on the website (such as the "Contact Us" form, newsletter signups, or donation portals) must rely on explicit, unambiguous, and opt-in consent48. Pre-ticked consent boxes are strictly illegal under the new framework.  
> 2. **Data Minimization and Retention (Articles 13, 18):** The church must only collect data strictly necessary for its stated pastoral or administrative functions. Crucially, data cannot be kept indefinitely; it must be securely and permanently destroyed when the retention period expires48. Tossing physical or digital records without certified destruction protocols is a direct violation49.  
> 3. **Confidentiality, Security, and Non-Discrimination (Articles 7, 15, 17):** The Parish Council must ensure technical protection (such as SSL encryption, restricted access protocols, and strong passwords) to prevent data breaches49. Furthermore, data must be processed without discrimination based on ethnic origin, regional affiliation, or political opinion50.  
> 4. **Breach Notification (Article 22):** In the event of a data hack or accidental leak, the church is legally obligated to notify the newly created Autorité de Protection des Données à Caractère Personnel (APDP) and the affected congregants immediately48.  
> 5. **DPO Appointment:** Depending on the scale of data processed, the church may be required to designate a Délégué à la Protection des Données (DPO) to oversee compliance and act as a liaison with the APDP48.
